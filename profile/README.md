## 🛠️ About Us
We are a community-driven organization dedicated to building open-source projects for embedded systems, Internet of Things (IoT), and smart hardware. Whether you are tinkering with ESP32s, sensors, or custom electronics, you'll find something exciting here!

### 🌟 Featured Domains
- 📟 **Embedded Systems & IoT** (ESP32, Edge Computing)
- 💡 **Smart Automation** (LED controllers, smart alarms, doorbells)
- 🎛️ **Hardware & Modules** 

## 🚀 Active Projects
- 🚪 **door-bell**: Modern smart doorbell firmware.
- 🚥 **dj-remix-leds**: Sound-reactive LED controller.
- 📏 **distance-alarm-380**: Proximity sensing and alerting module.

## 🤝 Let's Collaborate!
We welcome contributions from makers, hardware hackers, and firmware engineers! Check out our repositories, look for issues, and feel free to fork and submit Pull Requests.
